# Jefferson's Adventures

A cozy little platformer starring Jefferson, an orange tabby who is a bit derpy, loves
everyone, and takes his naps very seriously.

### ▶ [Play in your browser](https://jf1shh.github.io/jefferson-adventures-play/)

It runs on phones, tablets and computers. There's nothing to install and no account.

## The adventures

Every adventure ends at a sunny nap spot. Getting there finishes the level.

| # | Adventure | Where | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning Stretch | Living room | Collect 5 treats and reach the sunbeam |
| 2 | The Mug Situation | Kitchen counter | Knock 3 mugs off without being seen |
| 3 | Curtain Climb | Bedroom | Climb to the hidden treat on the top shelf |
| 4 | Vacuum Day | Hallway | Get past the vacuum to the laundry basket |
| 5 | Mouse Patrol | Backyard at dusk | Catch 3 mice while the coyote prowls |
| 6 | Roof Nap | Roof at sunset | Collect the golden treats and nap |

Finishing one adventure unlocks the next. Each one also has:

- **3 golden treats**, the stars on the select screen. They're optional, for replays.
- **A secret** to find. The first one is his favourite stuffed fish.
- **A best time** to beat. Missing it costs nothing.
- **An "oops" tally.** Jefferson never loses lives. When he gets into trouble, a human scoops
  him up and puts him back at the last checkpoint.

## Meet Jefferson

- **Derpy.** Leave him idle and he bleps (tongue out). A tumble leaves him dizzy-eyed.
- **Very social.** Friendly humans stand around the house. Stop next to one (or press ↓ /
  tap ▼) for pets. Pets refill his climbing stamina and count as a checkpoint.
- **Expert mouse hunter.** Level 5 is all about mice, and a mouse hides in each of the earlier
  levels too. The end credits keep a lifetime count.
- **His nemesis, the coyote.** It's goofy and lanky, and it can't climb, so the fence is safe.
  A pounce bonks it.
- **His stuffed fish.** He naps curled around it at every nap spot.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | ← → or A D | ◀ ▶ | D-pad or stick |
| Jump (hold to go higher) | Space, ↑ or W | JUMP or ▲ | A |
| Climb curtains and posts | hold Jump while touching | hold JUMP | hold A |
| Let go or hop off | ↓, or ← → | ▼, or ◀ ▶ | down or sideways |
| Pounce and swat | Z, J or X | SWAT | X, B or Y |
| Sprint | hold Shift or K | hold RUN | shoulder buttons |
| Hop down through a cushion | ↓ + Jump | ▼ + JUMP | down + A |
| Pause / restart / mute | Esc or P / R / M | II button | Start / Select |

Menus work with a mouse, touch, the Tab and arrow keys, or a gamepad (A to choose, B to go
back).

## On phones and tablets

- The on-screen pads appear the first time you touch the screen.
- Portrait and landscape both work. In portrait the pads sit below the game. In landscape they
  sit over it and stay see-through.
- Sound starts after your first tap, because phone browsers don't allow sound before then. Mute
  it any time with the ♪ button or in Settings.
- The game pauses and goes quiet when you switch to another app.

## Settings

Sound effects, music, volume, touch controls (auto, on or off), vibration, reduced motion and
full screen. **Assist mode** gives endless climbing and calmer hazards, which is good for
younger players or a relaxed run.

## Saving and privacy

- Progress (unlocked levels, stars, secrets, best times, mice caught) is saved in your
  browser on your device. Clearing this site's data resets it. If your browser blocks
  storage, the game still runs; it just won't remember progress.
- The game makes **no network requests** once the page has loaded. It has no ads, analytics,
  tracking, cookies or accounts. A strict Content-Security-Policy enforces this.

## Play offline

The whole game is the single file [`index.html`](index.html), about 170 KB. To keep a copy, open
it here and use "Download raw file", then open it in any modern browser. It works without an internet connection.

## About the art and sound

Everything is drawn by the game's own code every frame, using simple shapes in Jefferson's
colours. There are no image files and no AI image generation. Sound effects and music are
synthesised in the browser. Art based on photos of the real Jefferson may replace the
drawings one day.
