# Jefferson's Adventures

A cozy platformer starring Jefferson, an orange tabby who is a bit derpy, loves everyone, and takes his naps seriously.

### [Play in your browser](https://jf1shh.github.io/jefferson-adventures-play/)

It runs on phones, tablets, and computers, with nothing to install and no account.

## The adventures

Every adventure ends at a cozy nap spot. Reaching it finishes the level.

### Chapter 1: At home

| # | Adventure | Where | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning Stretch | Living room | Collect 5 treats and reach the sunbeam |
| 2 | The Mug Situation | Kitchen counter | Knock 3 mugs off without being seen |
| 3 | Curtain Climb | Bedroom | Climb to the hidden treat on the top shelf |
| 4 | Vacuum Day | Hallway | Get past the vacuum to the laundry basket |
| 5 | Mouse Patrol | Backyard at dusk | Catch 3 mice while the coyote prowls |
| 6 | Roof Nap | Roof at sunset | Collect 3 golden treats and nap |

### Seasonal (playable all year)

| Season | Adventure | Where | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Halloween | Spooky Season | Porch and living room | Knock 3 tiny pumpkins off the rail and find the witch hat |
| Thanksgiving | Feast Day | Dining room | Knock 3 dinner rolls off the tables and find the pilgrim hat |

Finishing one story adventure unlocks the next. Each stage also has:

- 3 golden treats, shown as stars on the select screen, optional for replays.
- A secret to find, starting with his favourite stuffed fish, plus wearable witch and pilgrim hats.
- A best time to beat, with no penalty if you take your time.
- A rescue counter instead of lives or game overs. When Jefferson gets into trouble, a human scoops him up and sets him down at the last checkpoint.

## Meet Jefferson

- He bleps (sticks his tongue out) when standing idle, and tumbles leave him dizzy-eyed.
- Friendly humans and guests stand along the way. Stop beside one (or press Down / tap Down) for pets, which refill his stamina and set a checkpoint.
- Level 5 focuses on catching mice, and extra mice hide in earlier stages too.
- The coyote is goofy and lanky, and it cannot climb fences. A pounce bonks it back.
- A Sticker Book tracks 30 stickers across milestones and 6 get-well cards from friends in Chapter 3.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | Arrows or A D | Left / Right | D-pad or stick |
| Jump (hold to go higher) | Space, Up, or W | JUMP or Up | A |
| Climb curtains and posts | hold Jump while touching | hold JUMP | hold A |
| Let go or hop off | Down, or Left / Right | Down, or Left / Right | down or sideways |
| Pounce and swat | Z, J, or X | SWAT | X, B, or Y |
| Sprint | hold Shift or K | hold RUN | shoulder buttons |
| Hop down through a platform | Down + Jump | Down + JUMP | down + A |
| Pause / restart / mute | Esc or P / R / M | Pause / Sound buttons | Start / Select |

Menus work with a mouse, touch, Tab and arrow keys, or a gamepad (A to choose, B to go back).

## On phones and tablets

- On-screen touch pads appear on the first touch.
- Both portrait and landscape orientations work, and full screen rotates freely with your device. In portrait the pads sit below the game; in landscape they sit transparently over the corners.
- Audio unlocks after your first tap, because mobile browsers require a tap before playing sound. Mute any time with the sound button or in Settings.
- The game pauses and mutes automatically when you switch away from the tab or app.

## Settings and languages

Settings lets you adjust sound effects, music, volume, touch controls (auto, on, or off), vibration, reduced motion, full screen, unlocked hats (none, witch hat, or pilgrim hat), and language. Assist mode gives endless climbing and calmer hazards for younger players or a relaxed run.

You can switch between English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Simplified Chinese, and Arabic (with full right-to-left layout).

## Saving and privacy

- Progress saves in your browser on your device. Clearing site data resets it, and the game still runs if storage is blocked.
- The game makes no network requests once loaded. It has no ads, analytics, tracking, cookies, or accounts, enforced by a strict Content-Security-Policy.

## Play a local copy

The game lives in the single file [`index.html`](index.html), roughly 600 KB. You can download it from this repository and open it directly in a modern browser with no install or build step.

## About the art and sound

Every background, character, prop, and sound effect is drawn and synthesised in code inside `index.html`. No photos of the real Jefferson and no external image or audio files are used.
